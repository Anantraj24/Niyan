# Brag Plan: NIYAM-X

## What is this app?
NIYAM-X is a sovereign adaptive mathematical optimization engine engineered for large-scale industrial decision-making under uncertainty, combining C++20 and NVIDIA CUDA execution with automated Model X-Ray diagnostics, DeltaSolve warm-restarts, and independent verification.

## The angle
Industrial optimization shouldn't be a fragile black box that breaks when market parameters shift. NIYAM-X introduces an explainable, 4-stage sovereign workflow: Diagnose the model, solve with GPU acceleration, adapt instantly to operational disruptions with DeltaSolve, and verify mathematically in an isolated sandbox.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The video opens on high-stakes industrial infrastructure (petrochemical refineries, power grids, logistics networks) overlaid with live mathematical constraint equations, converging into the glowing NIYAM-X Sovereign Engine Shield.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Model X-Ray & Structural Radar:** Probing matrix conditioning, ill-scaled rows, and extracting an explainable 7D Model Fingerprint.
- **C++20 & CUDA PDHG Numerical Core:** Accelerated sparse matrix-vector multiplication with live logarithmic residual convergence down to 1e-7 tolerance.
- **DeltaSolve & Scenario Swarm:** Preserving primal and dual states across parameter shocks (+8% crude price, +4% demand) to re-solve in 80% fewer iterations.
- **Independent Verifier & Proof Pack:** Decoupled audit sandbox validating all constraints and objective reconstruction without trusting solver status flags.

## Outro / punchline
"NIYAM-X: Diagnose the model. Solve intelligently. Adapt to reality. Verify the decision. Sovereign optimization for modern industry." (Smart India Hackathon 2026, Problem Statement 26119, Team TRINETRA 05).

## User flow worth showing
1. **Model Ingestion & Diagnosis:** Uploading `refinery_demo.mps` (25k vars, 18k constrs, 210k nonzeros), generating the Model X-Ray diagnostic radar and deterministic Autopilot dispatch.
2. **Accelerated Solving & Yield Synthesis:** Live GPU convergence visualization leading to the optimal refinery production slate (ULSD, Gasoline, Jet-A1, LPG, ₹31.82 Cr).
3. **Disruption Adaptation & Proof Pack:** Triggering an operational shock, observing DeltaSolve warm convergence to ₹29.91 Cr, and viewing the certified Proof Pack audit.
